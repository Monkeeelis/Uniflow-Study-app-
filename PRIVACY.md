# UniFlow privacy policy

**Last updated 21 August 2026**

---

## The short version

- No account, no password, no email address.
- Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising — there is no tracking code in this app.
- What you type stays on the UniFlow server, tied to this browser only.
- You can see, change or delete all of it at any time, without asking anyone.

---

## 1. Who this covers

UniFlow is a student project, not a company. This page explains what happens to
the information you put into it.

> **Legal position:** UniFlow is run under the annual turnover threshold in
> section 6D of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), so the Act does not currently bind
> it. We follow the 13 Australian Privacy Principles as a matter of policy
> regardless. The reasoning is set out in
> [`docs/PRIVACY-COMPLIANCE.md`](docs/PRIVACY-COMPLIANCE.md).

## 2. What UniFlow collects

Only what you type in, plus one identifier the server needs:

- Your name and year level, from setup — a nickname is fine
- The subjects you add
- Tasks, calendar events, notes and flashcards — whatever you choose to put in them
- Study sessions: the date, start and end time, and length of each timer run
- A random device ID, stored in a cookie named `uniflow_device`

There is no email address, no password, no phone number, no location and no
access to your contacts, camera or microphone.

## 3. The device ID, and what it means for you

The first time you open UniFlow, the server generates a random ID and stores it
in a cookie that lasts five years. That ID is how the server finds your data.
Two things follow from that, and you should know both:

- **The cookie is the key.** Anyone using this browser can open UniFlow and see
  your data — there is no password stopping them.
- **Your data is tied to this browser.** A different browser or device, or
  clearing your cookies, gives you a fresh and empty UniFlow.

That is the trade for not having to make an account. It also means UniFlow is a
poor choice on a shared or public computer — see section 9.

## 4. How your information is used

Only to run the app for you: showing your tasks, drawing your calendar, counting
your streak and totalling your study time.

It is never sold, never shared with anyone else, never used for advertising, and
never used to train anything. UniFlow contains no analytics, no advertising code
and no third-party trackers.

## 5. Where it is stored

Your data is saved as a single plain-text JSON file on the server running
UniFlow — one file per browser. It is not encrypted.

> ⚠️ Treat it the way you would treat a document on a shared school drive: fine
> for schoolwork, not the place for passwords, ID numbers or medical details.

The public version at [uniflow.pythonanywhere.com](https://uniflow.pythonanywhere.com/)
runs on PythonAnywhere, whose servers are located overseas, in the United
States. If you run your own copy of UniFlow, your data stays wherever you run it.

## 6. Information sent to other services

One, and only one: this page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your IP
address and browser type reach Google's servers each time you open UniFlow.
**Nothing you type is ever included** — no tasks, notes, flashcards or study
times leave the UniFlow server.

That is the complete list. There are no other third parties involved: no
analytics, no advertising, no embedded widgets.

## 7. How long it is kept

Until you delete it. **Reset**, on the Profile page, wipes everything immediately
and permanently.

There is currently no automatic clean-up of data belonging to browsers that stop
visiting. We plan to add one, and this page will state the period when we do.

## 8. Seeing, changing and deleting your information

There is nothing to request and no form to fill in — it is all on screen already:

- **See it** — every field is visible on the page it belongs to
- **Change it** — edit anything in place; Profile holds your name, year level and subjects
- **Delete it** — remove individual items, or use Reset to remove everything at once

If something is wrong or out of date, simply correct it.

## 9. Using UniFlow safely

A short list, and the whole of it matters:

- Use a nickname if you would rather. UniFlow never checks your name.
- **Never** put passwords, bank details, Medicare numbers, tax file numbers or
  student ID numbers into notes, tasks or flashcards.
- Keep health details and other private information out of your notes.
- Do not record other people's personal information — phone numbers, addresses,
  or anything about a classmate you would not say to their face.
- Avoid shared or public computers, for the reason in section 3. If you must use
  one, open a private window, or press Reset before you walk away.
- Reset cannot be undone. Download anything you want to keep first.

## 10. If you are under 18

UniFlow is built for students, so many of the people using it are. That makes the
advice in section 9 matter more, not less.

If you are unsure whether something is safe to type in, the answer is to leave it
out. A parent, carer or teacher can help you decide.

## 11. Questions or complaints

Raise an issue on the [project's GitHub repository](https://github.com/Monkeeelis/Uniflow-Study-app-).

If you are in Australia and believe your privacy has been mishandled, you can
also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at
[oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au/).

## 12. Changes to this policy

This page is updated as UniFlow changes. The date at the top is when it was last
revised.
