UniFlow

Since 2026

An all-in-one study app that helps students manage their classes, plan their time, and prepare for exams — scheduling and calendar, a Pomodoro-style focus tool, study streaks, and AI-powered flashcards, notes, and quizzes, all in one place.

About

Who it's for: students

Why it exists: to help students track their classes and study schedule, and to support exam preparation using AI and other motivation methods (streaks, reminders).

When / where: usable anytime, from anywhere with an internet connection (for the AI-powered features).

Features


📅 Scheduling, calendar, and planning
⏱️ Pomodoro-style focus tool, plus a normal timer and stopwatch
🔥 Streaks — a calendar view on your profile showing hours studied over time
🧠 AI-generated flashcards to help revision
